NOVA DRIFT — a neon single-screen arena shooter. WASD or arrows to move, mouse to aim, click or Space to fire. Drifters chase, spinners orbit and shoot from wave 4, splitters divide in two when killed. Gold star shards build a combo multiplier up to x8; shield, spread shot, rapid fire and magnet power-ups drop from kills. Three hulls, ten waves, and the Void Core boss on wave 10 — destroy it to win. Synthesised WebAudio sound throughout, particles, screen shake, hit flash and a parallax starfield. No network, no audio files, nothing to install.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. `main.ts` is the entry the grader runs: it copies `game/` and `screenshots/` to `/output` and writes `dossier.json`.
